The congregation

ARMSCON is the annual cultural and academic festival of PGIMS. For three days the campus opens workshops, competitions, cultural nights, and Star Night to students from PGIMS and visiting colleges.

This wireframe is the map for that site: enter from the landing, wander posters, then register as a PGIMS student or as a guest from another college.
